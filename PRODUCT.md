# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Astro with static output (house default from the small-business-site-build engagement). No framework runtime shipped to visitors; vanilla JS for nav and motion. Deploy target: GitHub + Vercel unless the founders say otherwise.

## Users

Owners and managers of businesses (any size, small business is the natural core) whose website looks dated, loads slowly, or doesn't exist. They arrive deciding whether AW Digital Solutions can be trusted with their online presence. Usually on a phone or laptop, comparing a few options, short on time. Their job: understand what AW does, judge whether the craft is real, and start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

AW Digital Solutions is the studio of two co-founders, Alex and Will. Mission (founders' words, verbatim): **"Building and managing modern, fast, and accessible websites for all businesses."**

The site is the studio's own official website. Success = a qualified business owner sends a project inquiry through the contact form. The site itself is the primary proof of craft.

## Positioning

A two-person studio: the client works directly with the two people who design, build and look after the site. They both build *and manage* sites (ongoing care, not hand-off-and-vanish). Modern, fast and accessible are the three named standards.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services (derived from the mission; exact service names are the founders' to confirm): new website design and build; redesign of existing sites; ongoing website management.
- No physical location, no opening hours published. Do not add either.
- Contact: inquiry form (Formspree swap point) plus a clearly marked placeholder email. No phone number provided.
- Domain: undecided. Use a placeholder and flag it.
- Pages excluded by the founders: reviews/testimonials page, events page.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **AW Digital Solutions** (A = Alex, W = Will).
- Mission line above is verbatim and must survive.
- No existing logo, colours or fonts: identity is to be proposed.
- Brief-pinned interface requirements: experimental (non-standard) navigation, neumorphism as an accent on a muted base, motion as a core design element with full reduced-motion fallback.

## Evidence on Hand

- None. No client projects to show yet, no testimonials, no reviews, no awards, no years-in-business figure, no client counts, no pricing.
- Never fabricate any of these. No "trusted by", no logo walls, no star ratings, no stats about results, no case studies.
- Founders' surnames and individual roles were not given: use "Alex" and "Will", co-founders.

## Product Principles

1. The site is the proof: every claim of "modern, fast, accessible" must be literally true of this site.
2. Honest about being new: show method and standards, never borrowed credibility.
3. Two people, directly reachable: the voice is first-person plural, human, not agency-speak.
4. One action: start a project conversation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA is a stated product promise ("accessible"), so it is a hard floor: keyboard-complete experimental nav, focus trapping, visible non-shadow focus states, contrast verified on rendered pixels, prefers-reduced-motion honoured, works without JavaScript.
